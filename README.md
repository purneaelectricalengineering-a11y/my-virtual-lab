## ⚡ Overview
**My Virtual Lab** is an interactive, photorealistic digital twin of physical laboratory equipment. It gives students hands-on pre-lab experience: drag realistic patchcords between terminals, turn rotary potentiometers, read spring-inertia analog meters, and verify circuit theorems before working with live hardware.

### Key Features
- **Accurate Hardware Front Panels**: Laser-focused, distraction-free instrument panels with authentic 4mm banana sockets, needle-spring mechanics with damping bounce, and rotary knobs.
- **Natural Patchcord Physics**: Drag flexible wires with natural Bezier gravity sag, terminal boot caps, and live micro-sparks on contact.
- **Real-Life Circuit Calculations**: Exact theoretical formulas for Kirchhoff's Laws, Superposition, Thevenin, and Maximum Power Transfer ($P = V \times I$ with peak at $R_L = R_i$).
- **Custom Student Observation Table**: Students can auto-log meter readings or click **"Add Custom Row"** to type their own parameters and build a personalized table.
- **Downloadable PDF Lab Reports**: Export clean, professional laboratory observation reports with student credentials, circuit schematics, custom observation tables, and characteristic graphs.
- **Feedback & Collaboration Portal**:
  1. Highlighted testimonials and feedback from students and faculty.
  2. Built-in **Error & Discrepancy Reporting Form** for continuous calibration.
  3. **Request an Experiment Form** to suggest new lab topics to the contributor team.
